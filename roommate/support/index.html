Getting help

Something broken, missing, or just confusing? Email iperez2435@gmail.com. We read every message and respond within 24 hours — that includes reports of abusive content or behavior in a household's chat.

What Quarters is

Quarters is a private app for roommates: chore rotations with photo proof, shared bills tracked in one place, and one chat for the whole house. Bill tracking is informational — the app records who owes what and moves no money, as the Terms set out. Each household is invite-only — a join code and a household password, approved by the owner.

The full support page

The complete support page — how to join a household, how to turn notifications back on, and how to delete your account — lives at thequarters.app/support. That is the support URL the App Store listing declares and the one the app links to from Settings; it is kept in step with the app, so it is the page to read.
